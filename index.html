How do I swap SUI for another cryptocurrency?

  1. Set your pair and amount

    Open the dashboard, choose SUI as the sending asset and select your receiving coin. Review the available route, rate and minimum amount.

  2. Match the receiving network

    Copy the address from your destination wallet on the selected network. Check the asset, address and expected amount before proceeding.

  3. Send SUI and track delivery

    Follow the order’s deposit instructions and leave SUI for gas. The service releases the asset after your SUI transaction confirms and the order is processed.

How to compare SUI swap quotes after fees

Sui’s low gas costs help keep the sending step inexpensive. Compare the final amount you receive to understand the full swap cost.

Sui gas
The network charge for sending SUI. Keep enough SUI outside your swap amount to cover it.
Swap cost
Service fees and the quoted exchange rate determine the swap cost. Check whether fees are included in your quote.
Delivery cost
A receiving-chain transaction adds its own network cost. Ethereum delivery can cost more than a transfer on Sui.

Why did my SUI swap fail?

Insufficient SUI for gas

Lower the amount you send so the wallet can also pay the transaction fee. Your full balance is not your spendable swap amount.

Quote expired or amount changed

Refresh the quote and check the new output. Follow the current order instructions before sending a deposit.

SUI sent, asset not received

Check the SUI transaction status first, then the order and payout status. For a confirmed deposit with no payout, contact the service with your order ID and transaction digest.

SUI swap essentials: rates, speed and receiving funds

Where can I swap SUI to USDC, USDT or ETH?

Open the swap dashboard, select SUI and your receiving asset, then check the available destination networks and quote. Match the receiving network to your wallet before creating the order.

How long does it take to swap SUI?

Sui confirms transactions quickly, but a complete swap also depends on the service’s processing and the receiving network. Track the deposit and payout separately using the order status and transaction details.

How much does it cost to swap SUI?

The total includes Sui gas, the service’s swap cost and any delivery-network charge. Review the quote’s fee breakdown and final receiving amount before sending SUI.

Can I swap SUI without keeping any SUI for gas?

For a normal wallet transfer, keep enough SUI to pay gas in addition to the amount you send. Reduce the swap amount if your wallet reports an insufficient balance.

Why does the quote change when I swap SUI?

Exchange rates and liquidity change between quotes. A floating-rate order can settle at a different rate; a fixed quote applies under its stated time and deposit conditions. Review the order’s rate terms before sending.
